Describe a home — where it is, how big, how old, what it comes with — and this gives you a ballpark of what it'd sell for today, as a single guess and as a range. It all runs right here in your browser, learned from about 3 million real sales (2012–2024) and then checked against years it had never seen. The honest part is the range, not the single number: it's tuned so that, for the wider band, about 4 in 5 real homes actually land inside it — a narrow range means the estimate is confident, a wide one means it isn't.
