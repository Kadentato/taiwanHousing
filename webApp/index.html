👋 Hey — welcome in

So — what do you actually want to know about Taiwan's housing?

By law, every home sale in Taiwan gets reported — millions of them, buried in a government data dump that isn't really built to be read. I cleaned the whole thing up and put it on a map you can click around. So pick a question, any question; I've already started a few of the good ones for you.

Does living near the MRT actually cost more?

It's a common assumption — so let's actually test it. Every Taipei and New Taipei home, coloured by how close it sits to the metro, with the lines laid right on top. The homes nearest a stop do tend to sell for more per ping; how much of that is really the metro is the interesting part.
