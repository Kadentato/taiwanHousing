👋 Hey — welcome in

So — what do you actually want to know about Taiwan's housing?

Every home sale in Taiwan gets reported by law — millions of them, buried in a government data dump that isn't really built to be read. I cleaned it up and put it on a map you can click around. Start simple, or jump straight to the mash-ups — I've teed up the good ones for you.
